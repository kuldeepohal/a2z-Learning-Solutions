Time Management for Students

By A2Z Expert Tutors August 29, 2026

Balancing school, competitive exam prep, hobbies, and social life can feel overwhelming. Good time management is the secret to handling it all without burning out.

1. The Pomodoro Technique

This is a game-changer. Study for 25 minutes with intense focus, then take a 5-minute break. After four cycles, take a longer 15-30 minute break. This keeps your brain fresh and prevents fatigue.

2. Time-Blocking

Instead of a long to-do list, schedule specific blocks of time for specific tasks. For example: 4 PM to 5 PM for Math homework, 5:15 PM to 6:15 PM for Physics revision.

3. Prioritize with the Eisenhower Matrix

  • Urgent and Important: Do it now (e.g., assignment due tomorrow).
  • Important but Not Urgent: Schedule it (e.g., long-term exam prep).
  • Urgent but Not Important: Delegate or minimize (e.g., responding to some emails).
  • Not Urgent and Not Important: Avoid (e.g., mindless scrolling).

4. Eliminate Distractions

When it's time to study, put your phone in another room or use apps that block social media. A distracted hour of studying is worth less than 20 minutes of focused work.
