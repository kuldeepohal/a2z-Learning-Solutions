Key Concepts to Remember

  • Chemistry: balance equations carefully and use redox basics where needed.
  • pH and salts: memorize acids, bases, salts, and their common properties.
  • Periodic trends: understand metals, non-metals, and valence-electron behavior.
  • Biology: learn life processes, reproduction, heredity, and evolution clearly.
  • Physics: use circuit diagrams and Ohm’s law to solve electricity questions.

Study Tips

  • NCERT first: revise examples and solved questions after every chapter.
  • Practice diagrams: eye, circuit, and plant physiology figures are very useful.
  • One-line notes: maintain quick notes for definitions, formulas, and terms.
  • Previous year questions: highlight repeated exam concepts and important patterns.

Final Revision Plan

  • End-of-day recap: revise all key terms and formulas once daily.
  • Mock practice: solve short questions and numerical problems consistently.
  • Keep diagrams ready: label them cleanly and practice written explanation.
  • Exam confidence: convert concepts into crisp, answer-ready points.
