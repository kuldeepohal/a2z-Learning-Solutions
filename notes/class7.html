Important Points

  • Food and survival: know how plants and animals obtain and use food.
  • Fibre and fabric: understand sources of fibre and how fabric is made.
  • Heat: learn heat transfer methods and temperature measurement.
  • Acids and bases: identify common examples and indicators.
  • Circuits: review simple circuit working, conductors, and insulators.

Study Tips

  • Use diagrams: revise nutrition, digestion, and light-related processes.
  • Flashcards: make quick cards for acids, bases, metals, and non-metals.
  • Short-answer practice: improve explanation and written wording.
  • Revision before tests: go over chapter summaries regularly.

Exam Approach

  • Notes first: keep a clean notebook with definitions and examples.
  • Daily revision: review short questions and concept words each evening.
  • Apply learning: connect topics to weather, home safety, and common ingredients.
  • Confidence: answer with one clear idea per point.

Recommended Study Add-ons

  • Revision notebook: keep one clean book for formulas, definitions, and diagram practice.
  • Practice workbook: use chapter drills and short questions to turn notes into recall.
  • Exam pack: add a compact set of summaries, checklists, and mock tests before exams.
