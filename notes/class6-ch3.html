Mindful Eating: A Path to a Healthy Body

📖 Learn — Study Notes

What is mindful eating?

Mindful eating means paying attention to what we eat and making thoughtful food choices. Food is not only about taste; it supplies the body with substances needed for growth, energy, repair and proper functioning.

🍚 Nutrients and their functions

Carbohydrates

Provide energy for daily activities.

Proteins

Help growth and repair of body tissues.

Fats

Provide energy and are part of a healthy diet when consumed appropriately.

Vitamins

Needed in small quantities for normal body functions and maintaining health.

Minerals

Needed for important body processes and healthy development.

Water & fibre

Water is essential for body functions; fibre supports healthy digestion.

🥗 Balanced Diet

A balanced diet provides the body with the different nutrients it needs in suitable amounts. A healthy food pattern should include variety rather than relying on only one kind of food.

Think before choosing: variety + appropriate quantity + nutritional value + regular healthy habits.

🥬 Food sources

NutrientExamples of food sources
CarbohydratesRice, wheat, potatoes and other cereal/tuber foods
ProteinsPulses, beans, milk, eggs, nuts and other protein-rich foods
FatsOils, nuts, seeds, ghee and other fat-containing foods
Vitamins & mineralsFruits, vegetables, milk and other varied foods

⚠️ Deficiency and health

If the diet does not provide enough of a required nutrient over time, deficiency can affect health. The chapter encourages recognising the relationship between food, nutrients and the body's needs rather than treating one food as a complete solution.

Study habit: Learn the nutrient → function → food-source relationship, and use examples to explain it.

🧠 Food choices and mindful habits

  • Notice what you are eating instead of eating without attention.
  • Prefer a varied diet that supplies different nutrients.
  • Think about nutritional value, not only taste or appearance.
  • Maintain healthy eating habits and avoid treating highly processed or sugary foods as the main part of the diet.
  • Connect food choices with growth, energy, health and daily activity.
