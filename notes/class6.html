Study Focus

  • Balanced diet: understand food groups and their importance.
  • Materials: remember rough vs smooth, absorbent vs waterproof, and heat effects.
  • Water and air: know the water cycle, sources of water, and conservation.
  • Plants and habitats: identify plant parts, living organisms, and adaptation.
  • Electricity basics: practice simple circuits and conductor vs insulator examples.

Revision Strategy

  • Quick notes: make one-page summaries for every chapter.
  • Draw diagrams: water cycle, plant parts, shadows, and circuits.
  • Practice daily: revise short questions, definitions, and examples.
  • Test yourself: focus on food groups, habitats, and materials before exams.

Study Tips

  • Notebook habit: keep a neat list of key terms and formulas.
  • Daily life connection: relate topics to water saving, food habits, and electricity safety.
  • Write clearly: practice short answers in simple, precise language.
  • Finish with review: revise summaries at the end of each study session.

Recommended Study Add-ons

  • Revision notebook: keep one clean book for formulas, definitions, and diagram practice.
  • Practice workbook: use chapter drills and short questions to turn notes into recall.
  • Exam pack: add a compact set of summaries, checklists, and mock tests before exams.
