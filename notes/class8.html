What to Memorize

  • Scientific Method: Understanding investigative steps and scientific inquiry.
  • Microbiology: Key characteristics of the invisible living world.
  • Electricity: Magnetic and heating effects of electric current.
  • Matter & Solutions: Properties of elements, compounds, mixtures, and solutions.
  • Optics: Ray diagrams and concepts for mirrors and lenses.

Revision Strategy

  • Formula sheet: keep force, pressure, distance, and speed formulas ready.
  • Practice diagrams: plant cell, animal cell, and solar system sketches.
  • One-page summaries: add definitions, examples, and chapter notes.
  • Daily review: revise often so topics stay fresh before tests.

Smart Study Plan

  • Focus on concepts: spend time on mechanisms and examples, not only definitions.
  • Use diagrams: visual memory helps with cells, solar system, and circuit-like processes.
  • Write short answers: clear sentences make revision easier.
  • Test weekly: self-quiz each chapter to improve retention.

Recommended Study Add-ons

  • Revision notebook: keep one clean book for formulas, definitions, and diagram practice.
  • Practice workbook: use chapter drills and short questions to turn notes into recall.
  • Exam pack: add a compact set of summaries, checklists, and mock tests before exams.
