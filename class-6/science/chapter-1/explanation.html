The Wonderful World of Science

Science begins with curiosity. This chapter introduces Science as a way of thinking, observing, asking questions, testing ideas and learning from evidence.

Quick SummaryPractice MCQsTake Interactive Quiz

1. What is Science?

The chapter presents Science as a way of thinking, observing and doing things to understand the world around us and uncover the secrets of the universe.

Science is described as a big adventure: we ask questions, explore the world and try to understand how things work. The most important starting point is curiosity.

Key idea: Science is not limited to a laboratory. It is everywhereâ€”from tiny grains of sand and a leaf of grass to mountains, oceans, the kitchen, the playground and outer space.

2. Science is an Unending Jigsaw Puzzle

The chapter compares Science with a giant, unending jigsaw puzzle. Each new discovery adds another piece. Because every new piece of knowledge can lead to more questions, there is no final limit to what we can discover.

Sometimes a discovery shows that an earlier idea was in the wrong place. New discoveries can therefore change our understanding of the world.

3. How Science Finds Answers

The chapter uses a simple everyday problem: a pen stops writing. We first ask why. We may guess that the ink has finished, then open the pen and check the refill. If it is empty, the guess is supported. If it is not empty, we make another guess and test again.

Step 1 Observe something interesting or something you do not understand.
Step 2 Wonder about it and form a question.
Step 3 Guess a possible answer and test it through experiments or more observations.
Step 4 Analyse the results to see whether they answer the question.

4. Curiosity, Questions and Teamwork

Learning Science develops our ability to find solutions to bigger problems and explore more mysteries of the universe. The first and foremost requirement for learning Science well is to be curious and observe our surroundings keenly.

Science is also rarely done alone. Scientists around the world often work in large teams, and we too can ask friends to help when we cannot find an answer ourselves.
