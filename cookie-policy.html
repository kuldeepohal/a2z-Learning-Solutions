Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and how we use them

Last Updated: August 2024

1. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They help websites function smoothly and efficiently by storing information about your preferences and activities.

Cookies can be either temporary (session cookies) that are deleted when you close your browser, or persistent (permanent cookies) that remain on your device until they expire or are manually deleted.

2. How We Use Cookies

A2Z Learning Solutions uses cookies for the following purposes:

2.1 Essential/Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function properly:

  • Authentication and security purposes
  • Maintaining user sessions
  • Processing form submissions
  • Load balancing and site performance

2.2 Functional Cookies

These cookies enhance user experience:

  • Remembering user preferences and settings
  • Storing user comments and feedback
  • Retaining language and display preferences
  • Remembering login information

2.3 Analytical Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use our site:

  • Tracking page views and user behavior
  • Analyzing website traffic patterns
  • Measuring the effectiveness of content
  • Identifying popular pages and features

2.4 Marketing/Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be used for marketing purposes:

  • Displaying relevant advertisements
  • Tracking conversion rates
  • Measuring campaign effectiveness

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Cookie Type Purpose Duration
Session ID User session management Session (deleted when browser closes)
User Preferences Remember display and language settings 1 year
Analytics Track website usage and performance 1-2 years
Comments Data Store user comments in browser storage Persistent (until cleared)
Marketing Tracking Track advertisement effectiveness 6 months to 2 years

4. Third-Party Cookies

A2Z Learning Solutions may allow third-party service providers to place cookies on our site for:

  • Analytics and performance monitoring (Google Analytics)
  • Advertising and marketing purposes
  • Payment processing
  • Social media integration

These third parties have their own cookie policies and privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their policies directly.

5. Privacy and Data Protection

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Cookies used on our site do not store sensitive personal information such as passwords, payment card details, or health information. Any personally identifiable information is encrypted and protected in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Note on LocalStorage: In addition to cookies, we use browser LocalStorage (for storing comments) which is independent of cookies. LocalStorage persists until manually cleared from your browser settings.

6. Managing Cookies

You have control over cookies on your device:

6.1 Browser Settings

Most browsers allow you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Block all cookies
  • Allow cookies with conditions
  • Delete cookies from your device

For instructions on managing cookies in your browser, please refer to your browser's help documentation.

6.2 Cookie Preferences

While we do not currently provide a dedicated cookie preference center, you can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect functionality of certain features on our website.

6.3 Opt-Out Options

To opt out of certain tracking:

  • Use your browser's "Do Not Track" feature (if available)
  • Visit third-party opt-out pages for analytics services
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies periodically

7. Consequences of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies:

  • Some features of our website may not function properly
  • User preferences may not be saved
  • Comments section may not work as expected
  • The website experience may be reduced

We recommend keeping essential cookies enabled for optimal website functionality.

8. Security of Cookies

We take security seriously:

  • Sensitive cookies are marked as "Secure" and only transmitted over HTTPS
  • Session cookies have appropriate expiration times
  • Cookies are not used to transmit sensitive information
  • All data is encrypted in transit

9. Children's Privacy

A2Z Learning Solutions is designed for educational purposes for students of Classes 6 to 10. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. Parents should be aware of cookie usage on our website and monitor their child's online activities.

10. Changes to Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, legal requirements, or our practices. We will notify users of significant changes via email or prominent notice on our website.
